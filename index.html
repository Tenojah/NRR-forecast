How to use the tool (30 seconds)
  1. Enter your base. Starting ARR from existing customers, and the ARR renewing in the next 120 days. You can type 4.8M, 4800000 or 4,800,000.
  2. Set your base case. GRR is the share of starting ARR you keep after churn and downgrades. Expansion is upsell and cross-sell, as a share of starting ARR.
  3. Compare three scenarios. Prudent, Base and Ambitious show NRR and ARR in 12 months. The bridge explains where the movement comes from.
  4. Prepare the message. Use "What one point is worth" for the executive committee, then copy the summary.
